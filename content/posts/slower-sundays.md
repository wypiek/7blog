I left the phone in another room and made coffee. Nothing remarkable happened. That was what made it nice.

There was an afternoon with no particular shape, and I let it stay that way for a while.

## One thing at a time

I usually have a queue in my head: a project to finish, a message to answer, something I meant to read. Even the quiet moments become opportunities to get ahead.

This Sunday, I opened a notebook instead. I wrote down a few things I could see from the window. I drew a terrible picture of the cup. I didn't turn any of it into a plan.

> A little unclaimed time can be a useful thing to keep.

## Room to notice

Later, I went for a walk without headphones. The leaves had changed since the last time I'd paid attention. Someone was cooking something good. A dog was having an excellent day.

I came home with no progress to report and a slightly clearer head.

I'd like to leave room for more afternoons like that.
