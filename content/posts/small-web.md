Some of my favorite websites don't do very much. A page of notes. A collection of photographs. Someone's remarkably detailed account of repairing a kitchen radio.

They feel like finding a handwritten sign down a quiet side street.

## Something with a person behind it

The small web is full of people making places for themselves. Their sites have odd corners, favorite colors, pages about things that interest almost nobody else. That's the point.

I like knowing that someone chose the background. That the links are there because they care about the places they lead to. That a page can stay still long enough for me to finish reading it.

## Leave a door open

A personal website doesn't need a grand purpose. You can start with an introduction, a list of things you're learning, or a few photographs from a walk.

Give it an address. Add a link to a friend's site. Come back when you have something else to say.

The internet still has room for that. We can keep making a little more.
