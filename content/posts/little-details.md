I have a soft spot for a button that looks like it wants to be pressed.

Not everything needs to be flat and quiet. Sometimes a little depth tells you where to go. Sometimes a tiny highlight makes something ordinary feel cared for.

## Familiar things help

A folder looks like a place to keep something. A window gives the page an edge. A small label explains what will happen before you click.

These details do useful work. They carry a bit of shared memory, and make an unfamiliar place easier to explore.

## Care, at a small scale

The details I notice most aren't complicated. Text with enough room to breathe. A link that says where it leads. A keyboard focus ring you can actually see. An error page that helps you find your way home.

Care adds up through a lot of little choices.

I'd like this site to be full of them.
