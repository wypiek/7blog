I keep catching myself wanting to finish this website. Every margin settled. Every page polished. Every future idea anticipated.

But a personal site is something you tend. There will always be another thing to change.

## Start with a few seeds

A useful beginning might be one good post and an introduction. A few links. Enough navigation that a visitor can find their way back.

You can add the clever part later. You might discover you don't need it.

## Prune with care

It's easy to collect features because they're possible. An animation here, another panel there. Before long, the words are competing with the furniture.

I try to ask a small question: does this help someone feel at home, or help them find what they came for?

If it does, it can stay. If it doesn't, perhaps it belongs in the experiments folder.

## Let it grow

The nicest personal sites often look lived in. A new page beside an old one. A favorite detail that survived a redesign. A note saying something is still in progress.

There's room for a little wildness. It's your garden.
